Paid Media Performance Analysis & Campaign Optimization
A spreadsheet-based portfolio project analyzing quarterly paid media performance across Meta Ads and Google Ads, with GA4 sessions used to assess traffic trends. The workbook combines KPI reporting, channel comparisons, campaign-level diagnostics, budget reallocation scenario analysis, and prioritized optimization recommendations.
Project objectives
- Evaluate Q1–Q3 trends in spend, attributed revenue, purchases/conversions, ROAS, CPA, AOV, CTR, CPC, CPM, and conversion rate.
- Compare Meta Ads and Google Ads performance to understand channel mix and relative efficiency.
- Segment Meta campaigns into Scale, Fix / Test, and Cut / Pause tiers using transparent ROAS thresholds.
- Review Google campaign-type performance across Demand Gen, Performance Max, Search, and Shopping.
- Assess the relationship between paid clicks and GA4 sessions to flag potential measurement, landing-page, or traffic-quality issues.
- Translate findings into budget considerations and a prioritized 30–60–90 day action plan.

Tools and analytical techniques
- Microsoft Excel / spreadsheet-based analysis
- KPI calculations and quarter-over-quarter variance analysis
- Paid media channel and campaign performance comparison
- ROAS-based campaign segmentation
- Scenario analysis for budget reallocation
- Data-quality checks and business-oriented recommendations
Selected observations from the analysis
- From Q1 to Q3, total ad spend increased by approximately 37%, while platform-attributed revenue increased by approximately 22%. Blended ROAS declined from about 0.97x to 0.87x over the same period.
- Meta's share of total ad spend increased from approximately 27% in Q1 to 48% in Q3, while Meta ROAS remained near 0.56x in Q3.
- Google Search recorded approximately 1.80x Q3 ROAS, using about 17% of Google spend, making it a channel worth evaluating for further investment subject to account-level validation and business constraints.
- The Q3 Meta tiering classified 6 of 32 campaigns as scale candidates, 14 as fix/test candidates, and 12 as cut/pause candidates based on the workbook's ROAS thresholds. The cut/pause tier represented approximately 27% of Q3 Meta spend.
- Clicks increased by approximately 47% from Q1 to Q3, while GA4 sessions declined by approximately 26%. The click-to-session rate fell from roughly 87% to 44%, suggesting a measurement, landing-page, or traffic-quality issue that warrants investigation.
These are descriptive findings from the workbook, not proof of causation or realized business impact.
Methodology
1. Summarize paid media KPIs by quarter and calculate quarter-over-quarter changes.
2. Compare Meta and Google spend, attributed revenue, ROAS, CPA, CTR, and CPC.
3. Review Google performance by campaign type.
4. Classify Meta campaigns using the editable thresholds in Campaign Insights:
   - Scale: ROAS at or above 1.0x
   - Fix / Test: ROAS from 0.4x up to (but not including) 1.0x
   - Cut / Pause candidate: ROAS below 0.4x
5. Model an illustrative budget reallocation scenario using editable assumptions, then translate the results into recommendations.
Important data notes and limitations
- Revenue is platform-attributed and has not been reconciled to Shopify or a blended revenue source.
- The workbook does not specify the currency; no currency symbol is applied.
- A ROAS of 1.0x means attributed revenue equals ad spend. It does not establish profitability because gross margin and other costs are not included.
- The reallocation scenario is assumption-driven and illustrative, not a forecast or guaranteed outcome.
- The workbook flags repeated Meta campaign values across quarters and a duplicated Google Q1 Demand Gen row. Validate the original exports before relying on campaign-level conclusions.
- Platform conversions can be fractional due to attribution or reporting methodology.
